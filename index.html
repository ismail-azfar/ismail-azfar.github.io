AI Early-Warning System

Problem debt shows up in bank data weeks before someone misses rent: a falling balance, payday loans, bounced bills. This system spots it early. An XGBoost model scores each customer's risk every week and explains why they were flagged, what-if scenarios show which changes would lower the risk, and IBM Granite running locally writes a supportive action plan with safety rules so it never suggests more borrowing and always points to free help. Everything runs on IBM LinuxONE, so financial data never leaves the machine.

Rescue Scout

A cheap scout robot that goes into a building before firefighters do, finds people and reports where they are. The prototype is a toy RC car with one FPV camera and a £5 ultrasonic sensor, with no LiDAR and no map. NVIDIA Nemotron vision models on Nebius Token Factory read the camera and choose each move, a planner surveys the room and heads for open doors, and a ground-plane calibration turns the single camera into a distance sensor.

It found the casualty even when starting facing the wrong way, and left the room through an open door to spot a target in the hallway.

Tilt

A Mafia game played over a call against AI townsfolk who watch your face for tells. A lie-detection model trained on a published deception dataset feeds a Bayesian town: each AI player keeps a probability that each person is the mafia, trusts the detector only as far as it has proven itself on you, and votes to maximise the chance the mafia goes. Every character has its own ElevenLabs v4 voice with delivery cues, so the narrator whispers at night and players get sarcastic when they don't believe you.

94%Mafia caught with clear tells (9% without detector)
42%→21%Innocents voted out in placebo test
0.568AUC on 172 unseen people
59%Failed face-tracking frames we fixed

A placebo test with tell-free players caught the AI finding signal in noise and the detector leaking players' roles mid-game. Both are fixed, and a test fails if the leak returns.

  • Computer vision
  • Bayesian inference
  • Model evaluation
  • ElevenLabs v4

SPY Quantitative Backtesting Engine

A Python backtester for 15-minute SPY data that works through three strategy families: moving-average trend filters, ICT Judas Swing liquidity sweeps and Fair Value Gap entries. Every trade pays a 0.01% cost, and results are stress-tested with 1,000-path Monte Carlo resampling. The best variant buys the first touch of a Fair Value Gap in the 09:30–11:00 session.

Day Trader's Journal

A free, lightweight trading journal I built when I couldn't find one that wasn't clunky. Log a trade with entry, exit and notes, and it works out P/L and keeps a running win rate. You can attach chart screenshots to each trade and sketch setups in a separate notepad, all beside a live TradingView chart. Everything is saved in the browser, so there's no account and no server.

My first plan was a custom live chart. API rate limits, unreliable WebSockets and iframes that block screenshots sent me back to a simpler design that works.

  • Tailwind CSS
